# ScansCard: rehearsal notes

The dashboard's Scans screen with one finding open in the side sheet, and three small step pictures (`Steps.tsx`). All four play once when they scroll into view and end on their payoff, which is also their still under reduced motion.

- **Built from the dashboard's own components** (`shell-sidebar`, `shell-topbar`, `scan-list`, `scan-status-badge`, `badge`, `card`, `table`), with the real copy, in the page's theme (Daylight first): the page's tokens, the page's sky behind glass cards. Not compared with a rendered screenshot of the real dashboard, which could not be run here.
- **Hero story** (`ScansCard.tsx`, about 6s): the cursor clicks New scan, a Running row arrives at the top, it turns Succeeded and its findings appear (counts, tabs and the sidebar badge update), the cursor clicks the row, and the finding opens in the side sheet: the page dims and blurs behind it and the sheet slides in from the right (the dashboard's Sheet: header strip with severity, state and "1 of N", title, file path, tinted section titles, code evidence, the decision footer). The sheet follows a screenshot of the real one, in Daylight instead of Moonlight, and its text is made up.
- **The tour** (about 28s, wide design only): after the finding opens, the cursor closes the sheet and clicks through the sidebar. Repositories: the branch page, scrolled so "How this is decided" opens on its three checks under the "Clear to ship" answer. Overview: the summary strip, where to look first, recent scans, then it scrolls to the budget, the success ring and the activity chart, which draw in. Files: the Report tab is clicked and the other file types fold away. Runners: the three connected computers arrive one by one. The pages crossfade and the breadcrumb follows. The phone-width design tells only the Scans part.
- **Scene specs** came from the dashboard's source (repo-detail, scan-verdict-card, scan-overview, the files list, the Connections page), read by a research agent and then drawn by hand. Each scene was checked as a still at 1440px; the loop was run once end to end with no console errors. The pages' text is the dashboard's own wording where I could read it, and the data is made up.
- **Steps** (`Steps.tsx`, one row each beside its text, at 704px, from the dashboard's own screens via a second research agent): connect is the New scan dialog (a link is pasted, the "Scan acme/web-app" row is clicked, the repository card, branch field and review types appear, Start audit is clicked and reads "Starting…"); audit is a scan page while it runs (the info alert steps through "Cloning the repository", "Reading the code for security issues" and "Saving the report", the elapsed time ticks, the badge turns Succeeded, the alert folds away and the cursor opens "Run details"); report is the finished scan (the four summary cells roll in, the four findings arrive in the findings table, the cursor rests on the first row). Checked: each end state on a screenshot at 1440px, the loop of each in the browser, no console errors, and at phone width (500px, the narrowest here) nothing runs past the right edge. Not checked: a true 390px viewport and frame-by-frame motion.
- **Checked in the browser:** the beats run in order (sampled by polling the DOM), the end states match the posters, console clean. Not checked frame by frame: the cursor path and the Swap crossfades.

- **Started from:** `examples/linear` (wide hero) and `examples/fade-crop` in the stageshow skill: a window inset a gutter from the top and sides, fading out at its foot, no plate behind it because the page is the ground. No satellite: the finding sheet is the product's own side sheet, inside the window.
- **Changed:** the content comes from the dashboard (`apps/elise-frontend-web/DESIGN-SCREENS.md`, Scan list): repository with a muted owner, branch and commit, a status pill on every row (green, blue, red), findings as severity dots, a lock on private repositories, and the finding sheet with what could happen and how it gets fixed. Icons are the dashboard's own (lucide, stroke 1.5). The look is the Default theme, with the accent mapped to the dashboard's dark gray.
- **Compact:** below 640px wide the stage switches to a 358px design with an icon rail, repository and status only, and no sheet. It is a second design, not a squeeze.
- **Checked:** the wide and compact stills in Midnight and Daylight at 1280 and 500px (the narrowest this browser allows), console clean, no clipped text in the list at the compact width.
- **Not checked:** the skill's `film.mjs --gate` (its dependencies are not installed here), a real 390px viewport, Default, Greenfields and Moonlight with this card, and the card in a browser other than Chrome.
- **Theme:** the pictures follow the page theme (changed 2026-11-16): `.dl` sets no colours, the skies are `var(--sky)`, and the browser bar maps to `--popover` in `look.ts`. Preview one in all five themes, not only Daylight.
- **Known gaps:** only the arrow cursor is switched for the dark themes. Everything on the pictures is made up, and the caption under the hero says so.
